The installer sets FulDC++ up for you and can import settings from an existing DC++, ApexDC++ or StrongDC++ profile. The portable zip unpacks anywhere and keeps its settings next to FulDC.exe — handy on a USB stick, or alongside another install.

Coming from AirDC++? FulDC++ runs completely separately and, on first launch, offers to import your existing AirDC++ profile — favourites, downloads and settings — so you lose nothing. Both clients can be installed at the same time.
